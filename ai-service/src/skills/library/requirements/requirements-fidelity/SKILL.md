# Requirements Fidelity (DevDrop original)

## Preserve what was supplied
Copy supplied values exactly: names, titles, copy, prices, links, contact details. Do not paraphrase, correct, translate or embellish them. Keep the websiteType exactly as given.

## Do not invent
Leave missing optional values absent. Never create biographies, testimonials, statistics, awards, addresses, opening hours or client names. If a section would need facts that were not supplied, list it under constraints as content-pending instead of filling it.

## Assets
Map every uploaded image or video to its assetId exactly as labelled. Documents (resume, PDF) are source material or links, not media. Never invent an assetId or URL.

## Essential gaps
If something essential for the stated goal is missing (for example a store with no products), record it in constraints and plan neutral placeholder structure that is clearly generic. Do not block the request.

## Constraints are binding
Treat the user's stated preferences, stack, sections and features as requirements. Skill or style guidance may refine how they are met but never removes or replaces them.
